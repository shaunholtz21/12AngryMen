Week 1 Recap

2026 Season

Hooligans (95.06) vs. 🔥 SHOULDA PLAYED THE OTHER GUY (122.54)

Result: Shoulda Played The Other Guy wins by 27.48
Mood: One team brought a bell cow, the other brought two of them

Shoulda Played The Other Guy got exactly the start they wanted — Derrick Henry went off for 35.3 and Jonathan Taylor added 25.1, more than papering over a rough Bo Nix outing (5.44 on a 16.1 projection).

Hooligans got nothing from their receivers — Davante Adams and Drake London combined for 11.1 points — and the Browns D/ST posted a negative score. Javonte Williams (24.2) was the lone bright spot in an otherwise forgettable lineup.

One team rode two workhorse backs to a rout. The other watched their receivers no-show and their defense go negative.

Bench Regrets:
Hooligans: Juwan Johnson (14.4) outscored starting TE Tyler Warren, and Meyers, Jones, and Shakir all outscored starting receivers.
Shoulda Played The Other Guy: No major regrets.

Shoulda Played The Other Guy didn't just win — they made it look easy from the opening kick.

🔐 THE PRISON WALLETS (154.42) vs. The Inevitable Champions (126.0)

Result: The Prison Wallets win by 28.42
Mood: One team broke the projection model, the other broke it on the bench instead

The Prison Wallets got a monster floor from Bijan Robinson (31.3) and an absurd 32.7 from Christian Watson — nearly triple his projection — enough to overcome quiet days from Sutton and Dicker.

The Inevitable Champions had real thunder of their own with D'Andre Swift (32.4) and Jaxon Smith-Njigba (26.2), but it didn't matter with Caleb Williams dropping 37.26 on the bench and Jalen Coker adding another 33.8 there.

One team's stars just outscored the other's. The other's best players were sitting on the bench the whole time.

Bench Regrets:
The Prison Wallets: No major regrets.
The Inevitable Champions: Caleb Williams (37.26) and Jalen Coker (33.8) combined for more points than half the starting lineup.

The Prison Wallets won with star power at the exact right positions.

Dirty Beers (89.82) vs. 🧱 THE MAX (107.96)

Result: The Max wins by 18.14
Mood: One team let Josh Allen do the talking, the other couldn't get a word in

The Max rode a monster 35.66 from Josh Allen — nearly double his projection — plus a sneaky 20.3 from Bucky Irving out of the FLEX, enough to cover for a quiet day from Puka Nacua and a total zero from Kyle Pitts.

Dirty Beers got a bright spot from Dallas Goedert (23.7), but A.J. Brown mustered just 5.6 before landing on IR, and the bench told its own story — Kyler Murray posted a negative score and Jonah Coleman zeroed out entirely.

One team's QB nearly doubled his projection. The other's backup QB somehow scored less than zero.

Bench Regrets:
Dirty Beers: No major regrets — the bench was as quiet as the starters.
The Max: Stefon Diggs (15.5) outscored a couple of starters, but nothing that changes the story.

The Max won because Josh Allen decided the game wasn't going to be close.

🧨 HUPHERS (119.16) vs. team spreitzer (104.7)

Result: HuPhers wins by 14.46
Mood: One team's receivers showed up together, the other's QB didn't show up at all

HuPhers got 45.2 combined points out of Zay Flowers and Ladd McConkey, and a steady 21.66 from Mahomes was enough to cover for a quiet day from the running backs.

team spreitzer had two real weapons in David Montgomery (28.9) and Trey McBride (24.5), but Matthew Stafford completely cratered — 4.1 points on a 17.5 projection — and Waddle managed just 1.2.

One team's receiver room carried the day. The other's quarterback forgot how to play football.

Bench Regrets:
HuPhers: No major regrets.
team spreitzer: Bryce Young (31.44) dropped a spike week from the bench that would've flipped this matchup outright.

HuPhers won on the strength of two receivers who refused to be quiet.

🦵 TEAM HANKSON (157.96) vs. Team Baune (80.86)

Result: Team Hankson wins by 77.1
Mood: One team had the best week in the league, the other had the worst

Team Hankson put together a wire-to-wire beatdown — Ashton Jeanty exploded for 32.7, Chris Olave nearly doubled his projection with 28.2, and even the Steelers D/ST chipped in 19 points. Ja'Marr Chase (3.2) was the only dud, and it didn't matter one bit.

Team Baune got a monster 31.2 from Justin Jefferson, but everything else fell apart — Achane, Judkins, and Dowdle all disappointed, and the Texans D/ST posted a negative score.

One team had almost nothing go wrong. The other had almost nothing go right.

Bench Regrets:
Team Hankson: No major regrets — the bench was loaded but didn't need to play a factor.
Team Baune: Jaxson Dart (26.6) and Isaiah Likely (27.8) combined outscored more than half the starting lineup.

Team Hankson posted the week's biggest blowout without breaking a sweat.

Team Corbett (88.76) vs. 🌿 TEAM GREENWELL (156.3)

Result: Team Greenwell wins by 67.54
Mood: One team had a monster week, the other just watched it happen

Team Greenwell was an absolute machine — Kenneth Walker III exploded for 34.1 out of the FLEX (more than double his projection), Jahmyr Gibbs added 33.6, and Trevor Lawrence chipped in 26.1, all despite a zero from Colston Loveland and the Chargers D/ST.

Team Corbett got a great day from Amon-Ra St. Brown (28.7), but Barkley, Herbert, and Kittle all fell well short of projections.

One team's floor was higher than the other's ceiling.

Bench Regrets:
Team Corbett: Jared Goff (16.44) and T.J. Hockenson (13.6) combined outscored three different starters.
Team Greenwell: No major regrets.

Team Greenwell delivered the week's biggest blowout on the other side of the bracket.
