Week 3 Recap

2026 Season

💥 HOOLIGANS (141.58) vs. Team Baune (87.18)

Result: Hooligans win by 54.4
Mood: One team got contributions everywhere, the other's bench outscored the starters

Hooligans got production up and down the roster — Drake London erupted for 28.4, Davante Adams added 20.7, and McCaffrey (21.6) and Javonte Williams (18.3) both handled business.

Team Baune fell apart at the skill positions — Justin Jefferson managed just 5.2 on an 18.3 projection, an injured Achane was limited to 1.7, and Isaiah Likely barely registered (3.3).

One team had no weak links. The other's actual bench outscored its own starting lineup.

Bench Regrets:
Team Baune: Harold Fannin Jr. (24.1), Kenyon Sadiq (23.5), and Kalif Raymond (21.0) combined for 88.4 points — more than the entire starting lineup managed.
Hooligans: A loaded bench (Juwan Johnson 23.3, Jakobi Meyers 19.4) that was never needed.

Hooligans delivered the week's most complete performance, start to finish.

🔐 THE PRISON WALLETS (135.22) vs. The Max (101.36)

Result: The Prison Wallets win by 33.86
Mood: One team had three stars show up, the other had one and a lot of quiet

The Prison Wallets got a monster 35.3 from Bijan Robinson, and Kyren Williams (21.8) plus Christian Watson (22.6) gave them three separate big performances.

The Max got a nice bounce-back from Jeremiyah Love (21.9), but Josh Allen was well off his projection (17.46 vs. 23.6), and both wide receivers came in under 9 points.

One team had three players go off in the same week. The other had one, and everyone else went quiet.

Bench Regrets:
The Max: George Holani (10.8) outscored a couple of starting backs.
The Prison Wallets: Matthew Golden (21.0) sat unused — would've made this even more lopsided.

The Prison Wallets won going away, three stars deep.

🌿 TEAM GREENWELL (146.28) vs. Team Hankson (125.04)

Result: Team Greenwell wins by 21.24
Mood: One team's RB duo went nuclear, the other's bench had the answer sitting right there

Team Greenwell got a spectacular 41.4 from Jahmyr Gibbs — well over an already-high 25.3 projection — and Kenneth Walker III (21.3) gave them a dominant one-two punch at running back.

Team Hankson fought hard — Ja'Marr Chase (24.8) and Chris Olave (19.7) both had strong games — but Dalton Kincaid was a non-factor (3.8) and Ashton Jeanty fell short of his projection.

One team's running backs were simply unstoppable. The other's stars couldn't quite close the gap.

Bench Regrets:
Team Hankson: Tyler Shough (23.8) alone nearly matched the entire margin, and McLaurin (19.7) plus Deebo Samuel (15.4) added even more unused firepower.
Team Greenwell: A quiet bench, though Jordan Love (18.48) was sitting there too.

Team Greenwell won behind the best running back duo in the league right now.
