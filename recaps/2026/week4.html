Week 4 Recap

2026 Season

🍻 DIRTY BEERS (146.38) vs. Hooligans (125.08)

Result: Dirty Beers win by 21.3
Mood: One team's receivers went nuclear, the other's bench had the better lineup

Dirty Beers won this one through the receiver spots. Tetairoa McMillan put up 45.2 out of the FLEX on a 15.8 projection, CeeDee Lamb added 41.3, and Carnell Tate chipped in 21.5. That covered for a brutal running back group (Isaiah Davis 0.0, Kaleb Johnson 2.2) and a quiet Kyler Murray (11.48).

Hooligans got a huge 31.3 from Javonte Williams and a strong C.J. Stroud (23.08), but Davante Adams managed just 7.2 and McLaughlin's kicker line was 2.0.

One team's receivers combined for 108 points. The other's best receiver scored 14.6.

Bench Regrets:
Dirty Beers: Drake Maye (26.16) outscored starting QB Kyler Murray by nearly 15 points, though it didn't cost them.
Hooligans: Alvin Kamara (22.8), DK Metcalf (16.5), Khalil Shakir (13.2), and Tony Pollard (13.0) all outscored at least one starter, and Metcalf alone beat Adams by 9.3. A few smart swaps would have closed most of the gap.

Dirty Beers won on the strength of two receivers who each beat everything the other side had.

The Prison Wallets (132.72) vs. 🌿 TEAM GREENWELL (149.18)

Result: Team Greenwell wins by 16.46
Mood: One team had the better stars, the other had the better bench and didn't use it

Team Greenwell got monster games from Kenneth Walker III (30.9) and Nico Collins (30.8, double his 15.3 projection), plus useful lines from Loveland (11.7), Santos (11.0), and the Packers D/ST (10.0). That more than covered for a quiet Trevor Lawrence (13.08) and a Gibbs outing (17.7) well below his 26.1 projection.

The Prison Wallets got one of the best individual games of the week from Kyren Williams (36.7 on a 13.1 projection), with Bijan Robinson adding 27.7 and Spencer Shrader a 16-point kicker line. Kelce (3.5), Watson (7.7), and DJ Moore (2.7) all disappeared, and that was the gap.

One team's stars covered for each other. The other left 21 points sitting on the bench.

Bench Regrets:
The Prison Wallets: Romeo Doubs (23.8) beat every one of their starting receivers. Starting him over DJ Moore (2.7) alone adds about 21 points and flips the result.
Team Greenwell: Brenton Strange (16.5) outscored starting TE Colston Loveland (11.7), but it didn't matter in the end.

Team Greenwell survived a shootout and had a lineup choice to thank for it.

The Max (88.82) vs. 🎯 SHOULDA DRAFTED THE OTHER GUY (132.72)

Result: Shoulda Drafted The Other Guy wins by 43.9 — the week's biggest blowout
Mood: One team won on depth, the other lost on a lineup full of question marks

Shoulda Drafted The Other Guy didn't need one huge game. Jonathan Taylor (22.7), Sam LaPorta (22.4 on an 11.2 projection), Michael Wilson (16.5), Fairbairn (16.0), Henry (15.9), and Warren (15.6) all came in at or above projection.

The Max got a big game from Puka Nacua (27.7) and little else. Josh Allen (18.52) fell short of his 22.2 projection, Love (6.5), Skattebo (7.6), and Irving (6.1) combined for 20.2 points at the running back spots, and Schultz (2.9) and the Broncos D/ST (1.0) added to it.

One team had six starters hit. The other had one star and a pile of injury designations.

Bench Regrets:
The Max: Brian Robinson Jr. (25.7) outscored all three of their starting running backs. Kirk Cousins (22.6) beat starting QB Josh Allen by about 4, and Jameson Williams (16.2) beat Diggs by nearly 8.
Shoulda Drafted The Other Guy: Wan'Dale Robinson (11.0) and Kenny Gainwell (10.7) both beat Egbuka (3.3), but it cost them nothing.

Shoulda Drafted The Other Guy turned depth into the week's most lopsided result.

HuPhers (122.5) vs. 🧊 TEAM BAUNE (123.92)

Result: Team Baune wins by 1.42 — the closest game of the season so far
Mood: One team won by a hair, the other lost it on the bench

Team Baune got contributions up and down the lineup: Burrow (23.72), Nabers (23.2), Judkins (21.6 on a 12.5 projection), and Vele (17.4). Those covered for a dead FLEX slot (Kalif Raymond, 2.6) and just 5.0 from McPherson.

HuPhers got the week's best kicker line from Jake Bates (18.0) and big games from Zay Flowers (25.8) and Chase Brown (19.1), plus Kraft (16.5) at tight end. But Ladd McConkey went 0.0 from the FLEX spot, Wicks managed 4.8, and Mahomes (17.0) came in under his 18.7 projection.

One team won by 1.42 points. The other had 15.4 points sitting on its bench.

Bench Regrets:
HuPhers: Rome Odunze (15.4) and KC Concepcion (11.4) both sat on the bench while McConkey (0.0) and Wicks (4.8) started. Odunze in for McConkey alone flips the result with room to spare.
Team Baune: Isaiah Likely (13.6) outscored the 2.6 they got from Raymond in the FLEX. They didn't need it, but it would have made the game a lot less stressful.

Team Baune won the closest game of the year, and HuPhers' lineup deserves most of the blame.

FTP closed the week with a comfortable win and a few lucky depth-chart bounces.
