Week 2 Recap

2026 Season

💥 HOOLIGANS (138.64) vs. Team Corbett (104.08)

Result: Hooligans win by 34.56
Mood: One team had a receiver go nuclear, the other had their QB go cold

Hooligans got a stunning 39.5 from Davante Adams — nearly triple his projection — and a strong McCaffrey game (22.6) plus a 16-point kicker line from McLaughlin sealed it, even with Javonte Williams falling short.

Team Corbett got a monster 35.2 from Amon-Ra St. Brown, but Justin Herbert cratered (7.88 on a 19.5 projection), Saquon Barkley was nearly invisible (3.0), and the Lions D/ST went negative.

One team's receiver had the game of his life. The other's quarterback had the opposite.

Bench Regrets:
Hooligans: No major regrets.
Team Corbett: Jared Goff (29.78) and Tre Tucker (22.9) combined outscored more than half the starting lineup.

Hooligans let Adams do all the heavy lifting and never looked back.

🍻 DIRTY BEERS (110.12) vs. HuPhers (94.58)

Result: Dirty Beers win by 15.54
Mood: One team rode a receiver eruption, the other had three straight zeros

Dirty Beers got a massive 35.3 from CeeDee Lamb, plus a hot Patriots D/ST (20.0) and a strong Butker kicker game (16.0) — enough to overcome a rough Drake Maye day (8.02) and a quiet Goedert (1.4).

HuPhers got a strong Mahomes outing (28.98) and a solid flex game from Omarion Hampton (17.5), but McConkey, Odunze, and Kraft all disappointed, and Flowers, Harrison Jr., and Bell each posted a flat zero.

Bench Regrets:

HuPhers: Dontayvion Wicks (12.4) outscored starting TE Tucker Kraft.

Dirty Beers won on the back of the week's best receiver performance.
