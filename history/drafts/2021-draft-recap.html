📝 2021 Draft Recap

Season Kickoff • Draft Room Chaos

Twelve managers entered the draft room fresh off the Aaron Rodgers offseason saga and one of the most hyped rookie classes in years. Here's how the inaugural 2021 squads shaped up.

⚙️ The Jerminator – The Ultimate Buy-Low

Christian McCaffrey at pick 1 is the single boldest bet in the draft — he played just three games in 2020 but was fantasy's most dominant back when healthy, making this a true championship-or-bust swing. A.J. Brown and Cooper Kupp add proven receiver talent, and DeVonta Smith gives them rookie upside. Trey Lance in round 10 is a pure dynasty-style stash — he's not expected to start right away behind Garoppolo in San Francisco.

Projection: If McCaffrey stays on the field, nobody in this league beats this roster. That's the whole bet.

🍻 Dirty Beers – The Kyle Pitts Swing

Dalvin Cook at pick 2 is a top-tier, if not the top, running back pick this year after a career-best 2020. Josh Allen, fresh off a runner-up MVP finish, is a rising fantasy QB1. But the headline is Kyle Pitts at pick 50 — the highest-drafted tight end prospect in years, going off the board in the fourth round of a lot of leagues despite the well-worn "rookie tight ends don't produce" rule. Joe Burrow, returning from a torn ACL, is a solid value at QB2.

Projection: Cook and Allen form an elite floor. Pitts is the wild card that could make this roster special if he breaks the rookie-TE mold.

🧱 The Max – The Rookie Class Special

Alvin Kamara at pick 3 comes off tying the all-time single-game touchdown record, an easy top-five pick. Najee Harris at pick 22 is this year's unanimous top rookie — the presumed instant bell-cow in Pittsburgh now that James Conner's left for Arizona. Trevor Lawrence, the most hyped rookie QB since Andrew Luck, rounds out a roster built on star power at every stage of career.

Projection: Kamara is the proven floor, Harris is the rookie everyone wants, and Lawrence is a name-value QB2 lottery ticket.

⚓ Navy Bombers – The Ja'Marr Chase Reunion

Derrick Henry at pick 4 comes off a historic 2,000-yard season, an easy top-five pick. Nick Chubb adds a second proven workhorse. Ja'Marr Chase at pick 76 is the most electric rookie WR bet in the class — reunited with his old LSU quarterback Joe Burrow in a Bengals offense expected to push the ball downfield often. Aaron Rodgers, fresh off his very public offseason drama, anchors the QB room at a discount.

Projection: Henry and Chubb form a bruising RB1/RB2 combo. If the Chase-Burrow reunion clicks early, this roster has real upside on top of a strong floor.

🐕 The Dog House – Building Around Two Locks

Tyreek Hill at pick 5 and Patrick Mahomes at pick 29 gives them the league's most explosive, proven QB-WR combo from the reigning AFC champs. Antonio Gibson adds a real RB1 after a strong rookie season in Washington. The receiver room past Hill (Johnson, Cooks, Shenault) is solid depth without another true difference-maker.

Projection: The Hill-Mahomes stack alone gives this team a weekly ceiling nobody else can match.

💥 The Hooligans – Paying the Kelce Premium

Travis Kelce at pick 6 locks in the clear TE1 in football, and Keenan Allen gives them a reliable PPR machine at receiver. Javonte Williams adds real rookie RB upside after a tackle-breaking college tape that turned heads. Justin Herbert, coming off a Rookie of the Year campaign, anchors a strong QB room. The RB depth past Montgomery and Williams is thin.

Projection: Kelce's weekly floor at tight end is a real structural advantage. The running back room needs one more piece to round out.

🌿 Team Greenwell – The Fields Flier

Ezekiel Elliott and Joe Mixon give this team two proven, high-volume backs to anchor the roster. George Kittle locks down tight end, and Jalen Hurts adds real rushing floor at QB. Justin Fields in round 13 is a pure late-round speculation play — he fell further than expected on draft night and is expected to open the year behind Andy Dalton in Chicago, with no clear timeline for when he takes over.

Projection: Elliott and Mixon give this team a strong floor regardless of what Fields ends up doing. Anything from the rookie QB late is pure bonus.

🧊 Team Baune – Locking In Two Locks

Aaron Jones and Justin Jefferson give this team two of the safest, highest-floor picks in the entire draft — Jefferson coming off one of the best rookie receiving seasons ever. CeeDee Lamb and Josh Jacobs add further proven talent, and Jaylen Waddle brings rookie upside in a crowded Miami receiver room. Depth past the top five thins out fast.

Projection: About as safe a top-five as this draft offered. If Jacobs and Lamb both take the expected leap, this team contends.

🎤 Don Kiedic – Cashing In on the Rodgers Resolution

Davante Adams at pick 9 got a lot more valuable the moment Aaron Rodgers's will-he-stay-in-Green-Bay drama finally resolved this summer — Adams has averaged 22 PPR points per game since 2018 and is squarely back in the WR1 conversation now that his quarterback situation is settled. Calvin Ridley adds a second true WR1, and Lamar Jackson anchors the QB room. Deebo Samuel is an intriguing buy-low after a quiet 2020.

Projection: If the Rodgers-Adams connection picks up where it left off, this team has one of the safest floors in the league at receiver.

🧠 1112 Aaww Zek Zek – The Stefon Diggs Anchor

Stefon Diggs at pick 10 comes off a monster first season in Buffalo, and DK Metcalf gives them a second true difference-maker. Beyond that, this roster leans heavily on unproven or committee names — James Robinson (an undrafted breakout in 2020) and Miles Sanders carry real touch-share uncertainty, and Tom Brady in round 5 is the only proven veteran arm.

Projection: Diggs and Metcalf can carry this team on good weeks. The rest of the roster needs several bets to hit at once.

🔢 Team Hankson – Doubling Down on Bounce-Backs

Saquon Barkley at pick 11 and Jonathan Taylor at pick 14 gives them two backs with real question marks — Barkley's returning from a torn ACL, while Taylor's outlook took a hit when Colts QB Carson Wentz went down with a foot injury right around draft season. Tee Higgins and Odell Beckham Jr. add proven upside at receiver, and Dak Prescott, back from a gruesome ankle injury, rounds out the QB room.

Projection: Two high-ceiling backs with real injury and situational risk. If both bounce back clean, this team is scary.

🏔️ Thunder Bluff War Stompers – The Proven Vet Collection

Austin Ekeler at pick 12 is a proven PPR machine, and DeAndre Hopkins adds a true WR1. Amari Cooper and D'Andre Swift round out a solid top four. Russell Wilson anchors the QB room, and Mark Andrews is a nice value at tight end. Tua Tagovailoa as a round-13 QB2 flier is a bet on year-two growth in Miami.

Projection: Solid, proven talent across the board with no glaring weakness. A steady, well-built roster from top to bottom.
