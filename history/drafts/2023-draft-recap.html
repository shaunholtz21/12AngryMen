📝 2023 Draft Recap

Season Kickoff • Draft Room Chaos

Twelve managers entered the draft room in a summer full of holdouts, blockbuster trades, and rookie running backs with more hype than some starting quarterbacks. Here's how the 2023 squads shaped up.

🔥 Dumpster Fire – The Safest Pick in the Draft

Justin Jefferson at pick 1 is about as close to a consensus #1 overall as fantasy football offers this year, and Jalen Hurts gives them a true dual-threat QB1 fresh off a Super Bowl appearance. Aaron Jones and Jonathan Taylor stack two capable, if injury-prone, backs. The receiver room past Jefferson thins out fast — Jeudy and Thomas are more WR3 than difference-maker at this stage of their careers.

Projection: Elite at the very top of the roster. If Jones and Taylor both stay on the field, this team has legitimate championship upside.

⚙️ The Jerminator – Riding the McCaffrey Wave

Christian McCaffrey at pick 2 comes off an absurd second half of 2022 after his trade to San Francisco — an RB1 overall in almost every ranking this summer. Joe Mixon and Waddle round out a strong top three, but the receiver room thins fast after that, and rostering two backup-caliber QBs (Tagovailoa, Jones) as depth feels more like a hedge than a plan.

Projection: If McCaffrey repeats his 49ers form for a full season, this team has as high a floor as anyone. The receiver corps needs a second option to emerge.

🧠 Team Corbett – The Bengals Stack

Ja'Marr Chase and Tee Higgins back-to-back gives them arguably the best WR1/WR2 combo in the draft, and DeVonta Smith adds a third legitimate target. George Kittle locks down tight end, but the running back room (Mattison, Akers, Dillon) is a committee of question marks, and Deshaun Watson — returning to a full season after his 2022 suspension — carries real uncertainty as the lone starting-caliber QB.

Projection: The receiver corps might be the best in the league. Everything hinges on whether Watson looks like himself in a full season back.

🔢 Team Hankson – Cashing In on the Ravens' New Offense

Austin Ekeler gives them a proven RB1 despite his own offseason contract drama in LA, and A.J. Brown is a locked-in WR1. But the headline pick is Lamar Jackson at 28 — fresh off a five-year, $260 million extension, with new OC Todd Monken and new weapons Odell Beckham Jr. and Zay Flowers promising a more pass-heavy, explosive Ravens offense. James Cook and Brian Robinson Jr. add solid RB depth.

Projection: If Monken's offense delivers on the hype, Jackson could have his best fantasy season yet. A well-rounded, high-upside build all around.

⚓ Navy Bombers – Chasing Last Year's Record-Breaker

Tyreek Hill at pick 5 comes off a historic 1,700-yard debut season in Miami, and Josh Jacobs — last year's NFL rushing leader — gives them a proven bell-cow, assuming his preseason contract holdout with the Raiders gets resolved in time. Chris Olave and Joe Burrow round out a strong top four. Aaron Rodgers at pick 92 is the wild card of the whole draft — a future Hall of Famer taking on a brand-new Jets offense after 18 years in Green Bay.

Projection: The top four is genuinely elite. Rodgers in New York is one of the biggest storylines in football — high risk, high reward as a late-round flier.

🌿 Team Greenwell – The Philly Anchor Plus the Wildest QB Swing

Saquon Barkley at pick 6 — even amid his own franchise-tag standoff with the Giants — gives them a true workhorse back, and Amon-Ra St. Brown adds a rock-solid WR1. Deebo Samuel brings a do-everything weapon, and Rhamondre Stevenson/Kenneth Walker III add real RB depth. Anthony Richardson at pick 102 is pure lottery ticket — the most athletically gifted QB in this year's rookie class, but also the rawest, with real accuracy concerns coming out of Florida.

Projection: A deep, talented roster at the skill positions. If Richardson's rushing upside translates to consistent QB2 production, this team has real sleeper potential.

🧨 HuPhers – Paying the Kelce Tax

Travis Kelce at pick 7 costs a premium but locks in the league's best weekly TE advantage. Nick Chubb gives them a true bell-cow RB1, and the receiver room (Cooper, Williams, Cooks, Dotson) is a lot of proven-but-unspectacular names. Justin Fields as the lone real starting QB is a bet on his legs translating to a sophomore breakout — the arm talent is still very much a question mark.

Projection: Kelce and Chubb form a strong floor at two premium positions. Fields needs to take the leap analysts have been projecting for this roster to really pop.

🧱 The Max – Betting on Pollard's Workhorse Role

Davante Adams anchors the receiver corps, and Tony Pollard gives them a real shot at a true bell-cow role after taking over Dallas's backfield down the stretch of 2022. DK Metcalf and Mark Andrews add proven ceiling, and Justin Herbert anchors the QB room. The running back room past Pollard (Herbert, Perine, McKinnon, Edwards-Helaire) is pure camp-body speculation.

Projection: Strong at receiver and quarterback. If Pollard gets the workload the Cowboys have hinted at, this roster has real teeth.

🍻 Dirty Beers – The Bijan Robinson Bet

Bijan Robinson at pick 9 was the single most talked-about pick of the draft — he's going off the board as high as #10 overall in a lot of rankings this summer, the most hyped rookie RB since Saquon Barkley, landing in an Atlanta offense built to run. CeeDee Lamb gives them a proven WR1, and grabbing both Trevor Lawrence and Dak Prescott secures the QB room. Two kickers is a choice nobody needed to make.

Projection: If Robinson lives up to the hype in that Falcons offense, this team has one of the highest ceilings in the league.

🌴 The Salamanders – The Buffalo Package, Plus a Rookie Riser

Stefon Diggs and Josh Allen back-to-back locks in one of the league's proven QB-WR1 stacks, and grabbing Jahmyr Gibbs at pick 39 gets them a piece of the most hyped rookie RB class in years — Gibbs is expected to split work in Detroit but with true home-run upside as a pass-catcher. Hockenson gives them a locked-in TE1. The RB room outside of Gibbs and Dobbins is speculative depth at best.

Projection: The Diggs-Allen stack alone can win shootouts, and Gibbs is the kind of rookie bet that pays off big if the touches materialize.

🧊 Team Baune – Paying Up for the Reigning Champ

Patrick Mahomes at pick 11 is a premium price for the reigning Super Bowl MVP, but Garrett Wilson gives them a legit young WR1 right after. Najee Harris and Travis Etienne Jr. form a two-back rotation with real upside, and DJ Moore lands in a new Chicago offense that's generating buzz after the Justin Fields weapons upgrade. The bench (Toney, Pickett) is speculative depth.

Projection: A strong, balanced core led by the league's best quarterback. Depth is thin, but the starting lineup can hang with anyone.

💥 Hooligans – Betting on a Healthy Kupp

Cooper Kupp at pick 12 is a discount on a 2021 Triple Crown winner — he's been battling a hamstring issue in camp after an ankle injury cut his 2022 short, so this is a calculated risk more than a reach. Derrick Henry gives them a proven workhorse, and Keenan Allen plus James Conner round out a solid top four. The QB room (Cousins, Geno Smith) is fine, not exciting, and depth thins out from there.

Projection: If Kupp's healthy by October, this team has one of the higher ceilings in the league. If the hamstring lingers, it's a rough start waiting on him.
